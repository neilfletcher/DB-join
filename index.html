Step 2

Join Archery GB

Everyone who shoots with us has to be a current member of Archery GB, the sport's governing body. It gives you your insurance, so nobody shoots without it. Archery GB membership used to be paid through the club; now you buy it yourself online and link it to Duston Bowmen.

  1. Go to the Archery GB membership portal

    Open agb.sport80.com. It's run by Sport:80 for Archery GB.

  2. Create your account

    Register with the same email address you gave us. If you've been an Archery GB member before, sign in or use "forgotten password" instead of making a second account.

  3. Buy your membership

    Choose the category that fits you: Senior (21 and over), Under 21, or Disabled.

  4. Choose Duston Bowmen as your club

    When it asks for your club, search for Duston Bowmen and pick it as your home (primary) club. Our county is Northamptonshire (NCAS) and our region is East Midlands (EMAS).

  5. Pay and get your number

    Your membership starts straight away and lasts 12 months. Your Archery GB number and membership card appear in the portal, and you'll get an email from Archery GB.

  6. Send us your number

    Use the form on this page, or reply to our welcome email. We'll approve your link to the club in Sport:80.

Sign in to the portal and add Duston Bowmen as your club (or make us your home club). Put your number on the application form and we'll check it.

    About us

    A club since 1954

    Duston Bowmen started in 1954 as the British Timken Bowmen, in the works canteen of the bearing factory at Duston, and took its current name in 2011. Our members have won national, regional and county titles, including Olympic medals in 1988 and 1992, and we welcome archers of every age and ability, from complete beginners to experienced competitors.
